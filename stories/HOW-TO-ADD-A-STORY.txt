Night stories: how to add one
=============================

Drop the files for a story into this folder, named <feeling>-<title>:

  alone-the-lighthouse.m4a   the recording (.m4a or .mp3)        required
  alone-the-lighthouse.png   one illustration (.png or .jpg)     optional
  alone-the-lighthouse.md    the text, for reading along         optional

The first line of the .md file can be the title, like "# The Lighthouse".
Without it, the title comes from the file name ("the-lighthouse" becomes
"The lighthouse").

Feelings (the first part of the name):
  alone, cant-be-myself, feel-too-much, not-enough, lost-who-i-was, angry,
  numb, scared-of-tomorrow, nobody-would-notice, missing-someone, failed,
  homesick

A feeling can hold several stories. A feeling with no recording shows a
"coming soon" card. The feelings' names and description lines are in
src/night/night.json.

Recording: a quiet room, the phone or microphone close, the same set-up for
every story so they sound like one voice. Export as .m4a or .mp3.

After adding files, commit and push: the app updates itself.
